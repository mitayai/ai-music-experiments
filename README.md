# AI music experiments

Songs generated locally on a single desktop PC with an open-source music model ([ACE-Step 1.5](https://github.com/ACE-Step/ACE-Step-1.5)), prompted and orchestrated through a conversation with an AI coding assistant (Claude Code). Every track here has its own page describing **what the human asked for**, **what prompt and settings the assistant actually sent to the model**, and **which tools and models were involved**.

The running theme is the physical hardware behind the cloud: data-centre racks, fans, failing disks and the people who look after them, taken for granted by everyone who says "it's in the cloud".

> **Note on copyright and AI generation.** These tracks were generated by an AI model, and the lyrics were drafted by an AI assistant from a human's idea. Under current guidance from the United States Copyright Office, material produced purely by AI, without sufficient human authorship, is not eligible for copyright protection. Other countries treat this differently and the law is still developing. This repository therefore makes **no claim that any track is protected by copyright**, and nothing here is legal advice. The AI model used is MIT-licensed, which places no restrictions on its output. Do your own checks before relying on any track commercially. No guarantee is given that a track doesn't resemble existing music. See [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md).

## Caveats

- The author of this repo (and the assistant that wrote its pages) has **not listened to the audio**. The assistant cannot hear. Checks were limited to file duration and loudness. Quality, vocal clarity and whether the sung words match the written lyrics are unverified.
- Vocals in this model are the weakest part: expect mumbled or altered words.
- The trance takes were requested as different styles from the futurepop track; the male-vocal trance song turned out to be the wrong style for the reference playlist and is kept for the record (see [process](docs/process.md)).
