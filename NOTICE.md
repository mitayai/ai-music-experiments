# Notice: AI-generated content

- All audio in this repository was generated by an AI music model (ACE-Step 1.5, MIT licence).
- All lyrics and all documentation pages were written by an AI assistant (Claude Code, Claude Sonnet 5.5) from prompts and ideas supplied by a human, who also chose, directed and reviewed the output.
- **This is not legal advice**, and the law on AI-generated works is unsettled and changing. The summary below was checked against public sources on 2026-11-09. Verify anything you rely on.

## Copyright status

### Canada (the author's jurisdiction)

- The *Copyright Act* does not expressly say whether works made by or with AI qualify for protection. The government's own consultation summary ("What We Heard", ISED, February 2025) states that under existing case law authorship "must be attributed to a human who exercises skill and judgment", and most stakeholders supported keeping human authorship central. The Canadian Bar Association's IP section has argued that the Act and case law point to human authorship being required, so works made entirely by AI would not qualify.
- Practice and litigation are not settled: the Canadian Intellectual Property Office does not examine the substance of registration claims, and it has reportedly registered at least one work naming an AI as co-author. That registration is being challenged in the Federal Court (CIPPIC v. Sahni, file T-1717-24). Sources disagree on how far that case has progressed, so check the court's docket.
- I found no new Canadian legislation or formal government policy adopting the consultation's recommendations.
- **What this means here:** the audio is machine-generated, so protection is doubtful. The lyrics were drafted by an AI from a human's idea; a human who writes or substantially edits lyrics has a much stronger claim over that human-authored part. **No copyright is claimed in this repository's audio or lyrics.**

### United States

- The US Copyright Office's position is that material produced purely by AI, without sufficient human authorship, is not eligible for copyright. Human contributions such as selection, arrangement or original editing can be protected.

### Elsewhere

- Other countries treat this differently and several are still deciding.

## Other cautions

- **Third-party similarity:** AI music models can produce output that resembles existing works. No originality check against existing music has been done. Some prompts name real artists as *style references only*; no samples, stems or melodies from them were used.
- **Licence of the tool:** ACE-Step is MIT-licensed, which does not restrict use of generated output. See [docs/tools-and-models.md](docs/tools-and-models.md).
- Anyone who reuses these tracks should disclose that they are AI-generated, in line with the model authors' own guidance.
